## Changed

- **Hybrid text rendering & emoji crash safety**: Replaced the fixed 256 KiB paged `hb-gpu` atlas cap with progressively growing atlas pages (up to 16 MiB) and growable workspaces, made color glyph effect outlines lazy, raised `MaxFontBytes` to 512 MiB, added a shelf-packed 2D RGBA `VulkanColorGlyphAtlas` for bitmap (`CBDT`/`sbix` PNG) and oversized emojis, and replaced unhandled glyph-encoding throws with deterministic fallbacks.
- **Two-stage shaping, caching, & free `ElementHandle`s**: Pooled `hb_buffer_t` and per-thread `hb_font_t` sub-fonts, eliminated `Substring`/`GCHandle` shaping copies, added `hb_font_get_nominal_glyph` fallback caching and ASCII fast paths, coalesced shaping-equivalent rich spans into `ShapedParagraph`, added byte-bounded run-shape and shared layout LRU caches with interval-width matching, and moved line display ranges onto `TextLine` while keeping `TextGeometry` lazy so `ElementHandle`s no longer bypass caches or trigger eager geometry builds.
- **Flat/pooled Vulkan text emission & retention**: Replaced per-glyph string hashing and 3x `atlasSet.FindIndex` scans with per-provider integer glyph tables and O(1) atlas slot hints, pooled segment storage without per-glyph validation arrays or `ResourceRefs`, hoisted `EmitGlyphPass` transforms/colors, broadened text retention to wrapped and undecorated rich text, and preserved resident SSBO subranges across mounts.
- **Windows shell & Acrylic cleanup**: Reverted `#925` (DirectComposition) and `#1037`, tracked per-HWND fullscreen state in `WindowsBackdrop` so fullscreen windows bypass caption/sizing hooks cleanly, and added `WM_GETMINMAXINFO` + `SHAppBarMessage` auto-hide taskbar insets aligned with `WM_NCCALCSIZE`.
