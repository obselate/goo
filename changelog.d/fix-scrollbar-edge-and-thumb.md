## Fixed

- Undecorated resizable windows: a scrollbar under the edge resize band now gets the pointer. The band still resizes where no scrollbar is underneath, and corners always resize.
- Scrollbar track and thumb both get `Hover` while the pointer is anywhere over the scrollbar. A thumb `Width` (vertical) or `Height` (horizontal) now makes it thinner than the track, centered, and hover or transition changes to it lay out again.
